GST calculator for GST 2.0 rates

This GST calculator adds GST to a price or removes GST from a GST-inclusive price, and shows the CGST + SGST split for sales within a state or IGST for inter-state sales. It uses the new GST 2.0 rate structure that came into force on 22 September 2025, when the GST Council replaced the old 5 / 12 / 18 / 28% slabs with two main rates — 5% and 18% — plus a special 40% rate for luxury and sin goods.

Rates depend on the exact HSN/SAC code of a product or service. Check the CBIC rate notification for your item before invoicing.

How to calculate GST

Adding GST to a price

GST amount = Net price × GST rate ÷ 100
Total price = Net price + GST amount

Example: a ₹10,000 service at 18% → GST = ₹1,800 → invoice total ₹11,800 (CGST ₹900 + SGST ₹900 within the state, or IGST ₹1,800 for another state).

Removing GST from an inclusive price (reverse GST)

Net price = Total price × 100 ÷ (100 + GST rate)
GST amount = Total price − Net price

Example: an item sold for ₹1,050 including 5% GST → net price ₹1,000 and GST ₹50. A common mistake is to take 5% of ₹1,050 (₹52.50) — that overstates the tax.

CGST, SGST and IGST explained

  • Intra-state sale (seller and buyer in the same state): GST is split equally into Central GST (CGST) and State GST (SGST) — or UTGST in a Union Territory.
  • Inter-state sale and imports: the full rate is charged as Integrated GST (IGST).
  • The total tax is the same in both cases; only the government that receives it changes.

What changed with GST 2.0?

  • The 12% slab was removed — most items moved down to 5%.
  • The 28% slab was removed — most items moved to 18%; luxury and sin goods moved to 40%.
  • Compensation cess was folded into the new 40% rate for most goods.
  • Individual life and health insurance premiums became GST-free.

Frequently asked questions

What are the GST rates in India now?

Since 22 September 2025 (GST 2.0) the main rates are 5% and 18%, with a special 40% rate for luxury and sin goods. Gold and silver are taxed at 3% and rough diamonds at 0.25%. Many essentials are exempt.

How do I remove GST from a total amount?

Divide the total by (1 + rate/100). For example, ₹1,180 including 18% GST: 1,180 ÷ 1.18 = ₹1,000 net price, so GST is ₹180.

Is there still a 12% or 28% GST slab?

No. Under GST 2.0 the 12% and 28% slabs were removed. Most 12% items moved to 5% and most 28% items moved to 18%, while some luxury and sin goods moved to 40%.

What is the difference between CGST, SGST and IGST?

For a sale within one state the GST is split half-and-half into CGST and SGST. For a sale between states, the full amount is charged as IGST. The total tax is the same.

Is GST charged on health insurance?

Individual health insurance and life insurance premiums are exempt from GST from 22 September 2025.
